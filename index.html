How to create your stream key

  1. Open YouTube Studio Go to studio.youtube.com with the account that will go live.
  2. Create → Go live Top right. First time, YouTube may ask you to verify your phone.
  3. Choose “Stream” Do not use YouTube’s webcam mode. BareCast needs an encoder stream key. Create the key with the HLS protocol.
  4. Copy the stream key It looks like xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx. Paste it on the right. It stays on this computer only.

The key is sent to youtubehsls.lonormal.com, then forwarded to YouTube. Paste it and enter the studio.
